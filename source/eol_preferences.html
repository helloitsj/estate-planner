End-of-life preferences — a sheet to talk through

Each person fills this in for themselves. These wishes guide a health care agent — typically a spouse, then a named backup chain — when you can't speak for yourself.

Educational information, not legal advice. This page describes how New York instruments generally operate. It is not a legal instrument, does not account for any individual situation, and law varies by state and changes over time. Consult a licensed New York attorney.

Mostly optional — with one exception. New York only requires that you name an agent; stating wishes is your choice. But your agent may make feeding-tube (artificial nutrition & hydration) decisions only if they reasonably know your wishes. Left blank, it ties an agent's hands on the decision that most often matters. At minimum, answer that row and add a short values note at the bottom.
