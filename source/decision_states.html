01 Document lifespans

Each instrument switches on and off at a specific trigger. Two are easy to get wrong: a POA and a healthcare proxy go active the moment you can't decide, and terminate the instant you die — they never survive death. A will does the opposite: dead weight until death, then it activates. Red edge marks a termination point.

02 Who holds each decision, state by state

Six states cover the full arc. The two shaded in brass are the most commonly misunderstood: alive but unable to decide, and the transitions where authority moves or ends.

— While you are alive —
S0

Both of you alive & competent

Everything is drafted but dormant; each spouse runs their own life.

Medical
Each of you decides for yourself.
Money / property
Each decides; you two are co-trustees of the trust and can amend or revoke it anytime.
The children
You parent them.

S1

Whichever of you can't decide, only that spouse's documents wake up. The setup is a mirror — it reads the same whichever spouse is incapacitated.

The well spouse decides for the incapacitated one, as that spouse's named primary healthcare agent.
Money / property
The well spouse acts as primary POA agent and carries the trust alone as sole trustee.

S2

Both incapacitated at once — alive, neither can decide

The shared-catastrophe case (accident, both in the ICU). This is the state the backup chains exist for.

Medical
Health agent 2 decides for each spouse → then health agent 3. One agent at a time; NY bars co-agents.
Money / property
POA agent 2 steps in → then POA agent 3. For the trust, the successor line takes over: trustee 2 → trustee 3 → trustee 4, with the trust protector overseeing.
The children
Standby guardian, and only if that designation exists: Guardian 1 → guardian 2 → guardian 3 — the same chain as the testamentary guardian, effective during joint incapacity. A will alone does not cover this (see flag).
Papers live
Both Proxies + POAsTrust — successor trusteeWills still dormant (no death)

The gap in this state: guardianship of the kids

A guardian named in your will only takes effect when you die — not while you're alive but incapacitated. To let the named guardian take custody in this exact situation, NY requires a separate standby-guardian designation (SCPA 1726), effective on incapacity. Without it, if you're both alive but unable to parent, no one has clean legal authority over the kids until a court acts. Draft it to run during joint incapacity, so it ends the moment either of you recovers — a loan of authority, not a transfer.

— At and after death —
S3

One of you dies, the other survives

Whichever spouse dies, their lifetime papers switch off and their will switches on. Same pattern in either direction.

Terminates now
Deceased spouse's Proxy — endsDeceased spouse's POA — ends Both die with them; an agent's authority never outlives the person.
Activates now
Deceased spouse's Will / Executor — the surviving spouse, then executor 2 → executor 3 → executor 4. The pour-over will sweeps any stray assets into the trust.
Medical / money
The surviving spouse decides for themselves and continues as sole trustee.
The children
The surviving spouse parents; no guardian needed while a parent lives.
S4

Both of you gone — children are minors

The scenario most plans are built around. Money and custody are often split between two people on purpose.

Terminated
All Proxies + POAs — ended Trust is now irrevocable.
Custody
Guardian 1 → guardian 2 → guardian 3. Raises the kids; does not fund them.
The money
Trustee 1 → trustee 2 → trustee 3 pays for the children from the trust under the HEMS standard — health, education, maintenance, support. The trust protector oversees.
Estates
Both Wills active Executors wind up probate and pour into the trust.
Note
If the first trustee is unavailable, one person can end up holding guardian and trustee together — an overlap worth deciding deliberately; a trust protector is the usual check.
S5

Both of you gone — children are adults

Custody is over; the trust is the only thing still running.

Terminates
Guardianship — ended at youngest's 18th No guardian exists once both kids are adults.
The money
Trustee 1 → trustee 2 → trustee 3 continue; distributions release on the schedule the trust sets. Kids may take over their own shares depending on the final distribution design.
Trust ends
Each child's share closes at their final distribution age — the last thing to switch off.
