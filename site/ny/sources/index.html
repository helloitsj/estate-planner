How to read this

  • As of is the date the value was last checked against the primary source, not the date it was published. These figures adjust — the estate-tax exclusions change most Januaries — so a stale date means confirm before relying on it.
  • Every value here was fetched from the primary source linked in its row. None is written from memory.
  • The machine-readable record is data/ny/figures.json, where each number lives once with its own verification date.

Educational information, not legal advice. Figures change — often every January. Each is shown with the date it was verified against its primary source. If a date is old, treat the number as stale and confirm it yourself or with a licensed New York attorney.
