Walk in prepared

A short walkthrough that turns a handful of answers into a printable agenda for your attorney — so a first meeting starts on your situation, not on definitions.

Educational information, not legal advice. This walkthrough never evaluates your situation or tells you what to do. Every path ends in questions to ask a licensed New York attorney, never a conclusion.

in progress The interactive walkthrough is being built. This page is a placeholder describing what it will do. In the meantime, the life-state timeline covers the same ground you'll be answering questions about.

What it will produce

Answer at most a handful of questions — no path longer than five — and the tool assembles a one-page agenda you can print and read across a table:

  • Household facts — the shape of your family, in your own words.
  • Fiduciary candidates — the people you'd name as agent, executor, trustee, or guardian, and the order.
  • An asset checklist — what exists to be planned around.
  • Questions for your attorney — generated from your answers, phrased as questions, plus facts worth confirming.

The privacy promise

Your answers are stored only in your own browser, using local storage. Nothing is transmitted, nothing is put in the web address, and there is no server to send it to. A clear-my-answers button wipes it completely, and you'll see a visible reminder that your answers never leave this browser.

What it won't do

It won't grade your plan, tell you what you need, or draft anything. It describes how the law works and hands the decisions that are yours to you and your attorney — as questions.
