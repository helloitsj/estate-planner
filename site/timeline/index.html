S1

One spouse incapacitated, the other well

Only the incapacitated spouse’s documents wake up. It reads the same whichever spouse it is.

Medical
The well spouse decides for the incapacitated one as primary health care agent.
Money / property
The well spouse acts as primary POA agent and carries the trust as sole trustee.
The children
The well spouse parents normally.

S2

Both incapacitated at once

Alive, neither can decide. This is the state the backup chains exist for.

Medical
Health agent 2 decides for each spouse, then health agent 3. One agent at a time — New York bars co-agents.
Money / property
POA agent 2, then agent 3. For the trust, the successor trustee line takes over.
The children
Only a standby guardian designation covers this. A will does not.

The gap in this state

A guardian named in a will takes effect at death — not while a parent is alive but incapacitated. New York requires a separate standby guardian designation (SCPA 1726) that operates on incapacity. Without one, nobody holds clean legal authority over the children until a court acts.

S3

One spouse dies, the other survives

The deceased spouse’s lifetime papers switch off and their will switches on.

Medical
The surviving spouse decides for themselves.
Money / property
The surviving spouse continues as sole trustee. The pour-over will sweeps stray assets into the trust.
The children
The surviving spouse parents. No guardian is needed while a parent lives.
Instruments
Proxy ended POA ended Will active Trust active Guardianship dormant
S4

Both parents gone — children are minors

Money and custody are often split between two people on purpose.

Medical
No agent exists. Authority ended at death.
Money / property
Trustee 1, then 2, then 3 pays from the trust under the HEMS standard — health, education, maintenance, support.
The children
Guardian 1, then 2, then 3 raises the children. The guardian does not control the money.
Instruments
Proxy ended POA ended Will active Trust irrevocable Guardianship active
S5

Both parents gone — children are adults

Custody is over. The trust is the only thing still running.

Medical
No agent exists.
Money / property
The trustee line continues. Distributions release on the schedule the trust sets.
The children
Guardianship ended at the youngest child’s eighteenth birthday.
Instruments
Proxy ended POA ended Will closed Trust active Guardianship ended
