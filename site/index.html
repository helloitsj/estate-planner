Who decides what, and when

Everything written about estate planning is organized by document. That's the wrong index. Nobody wonders what a power of attorney is — they wonder who signs for the apartment while they're in a coma, and whether that changes the moment they die.

Start where you actually are

The instruments work the same for everyone. What changes is who's in the chain by default, what happens if you do nothing, and which gaps your situation opens.

Married or partnered, minor children

Guardianship, the incapacity gap, and the pour-over will

Single parent

No surviving-parent default. Guardianship is everything.

Blended family

Stepchildren, elective share, stale beneficiary forms

Unmarried partners

No intestate rights. No default authority.
