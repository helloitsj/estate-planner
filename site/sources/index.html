Sources and last-verified dates

Every figure and citation this site relies on, with its primary source and the date it was last checked. Numbers are never written from memory.

Educational information, not legal advice. Figures change — often every January. Each is shown with the date it was verified against its primary source. If a date is old, treat the number as stale and confirm it yourself or with a licensed New York attorney.

This site prefers durable mechanisms over changeable numbers wherever it can — "a power of attorney terminates at death" needs no date. But some content genuinely depends on a figure, and every one of those lives in a single machine-readable file, data/figures.json, so a number appears in exactly one place and carries its own verification date.
